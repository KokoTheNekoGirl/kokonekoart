### What to expect

- Every project starts with a conversation about what you need. I review your requirements, current setup and any technical constraints, then send a scoped quote before development begins.
- I will share my progress at each stage (for example wireframes, design mockups, then a staging or development version), and you may ask for an update anytime, within reason.
- Early wireframe exploration is flexible. Once design mockups are approved, major layout or direction changes may require additional time. The same applies once development begins.
- I will check in before 80% of the planned time is used if new requirements, additions or feedback may affect the timeline or budget. Extra costs may apply if the scope expands or changes.
- You will not be charged for overtime caused by my own work.
- Your website or app launches only after final payment is received.
- You are expected to host and maintain the project yourself after launch. See [hosting and maintenance](#hosting-maintenance).

### My Rights

- I retain the right to refuse or cancel a project. See my [cancellation and refunds policy](#cancellation-refunds).
- I retain my copyrights of any unfinished or completed work. See my [ownership and rights policy](#ownership).

</TosSection>

### What I keep

- I retain the copyright to the designs, assets, and code I create for you.
- Your own content, logos, brand materials, or anything else you supply remain yours.
- I keep the right to display the finished project publicly once it is complete, such as in my portfolio, on my website, and as source code on GitHub. I never include customer data or logins on Git Repositories.
- If you need your project kept private, we can agree on that in writing, and I’ll share it once it’s publicly allowed.

### What you may not do

- You may not resell, redistribute or sublicense the work, its code or its assets, on their own or as templates.
- You may not use the work to train AI, or to alter or extend it using AI. Doing so risks future business relations.
- Third-party libraries, fonts, plugins and themes keep their own licenses.

### What to expect

- Annual service is paid upfront for the year, and monthly service is paid upfront for each month. Service does not renew automatically. If payment is late, your service is suspended until it is received. Unused time is non-refundable.
- I will remind you one full month before your term ends, and you can choose to renew at your current rate plus 1–2% for inflation, if applicable.
- Any rate adjustment is discussed in advance and applies only to a new renewal period.
- I monitor your site and keep backups, but I cannot guarantee uninterrupted uptime, successful restores, or that a site cannot be hacked. Outages at third-party providers are outside my control.
- You are responsible for keeping your own accounts and logins secure and for providing working admin access. I am not responsible for problems caused by edits or installs made by others.
- Very heavy traffic or resource use on hosted sites or bots may need a different plan.

### Monthly Maintenance

- Monthly maintenance covers core and security updates, backups, uptime and performance monitoring, and one hour per month of content changes and small fixes.
- Unused hours do not roll over. Work beyond the included hour is quoted separately.
- Monthly service has a default term of one year. You pay month to month and can cancel at any time. Alternative term lengths can be discussed.
- Your monthly rate is locked for the current service term.

### Annual Hosting and Management

- Annual hosting and management covers domain renewals, DNS management, basic DDoS protection through Cloudflare, and hosting renewals.
- Theme and plugin license renewals are not included.
- Includes monthly maintenance, plus 2 hours of edits and deployments each month. Unused hours do not roll over, and work beyond the included hours is quoted separately.

### Domains

- If you already own your domain, I need admin access to it to manage it.
- Domains I buy on your behalf are registered under my account. If you would like a domain in your name, or want to manage it yourself, it can be transferred to you.

### When Service Ends

- When your service ends, whether your term finishes or you cancel, hosting, monitoring, maintenance and protection (including Cloudflare protection) stop 7 days after the end of the period you have paid for. Your website or bot may go offline at that point.
- I will keep your backups and files for 90 days after the end date. After that they may be deleted, and I cannot guarantee they can be recovered.
- During those 90 days you can ask me for a copy of your files, or renew and have your service restored from them.
- If I manage your domain or DNS, contact me before the term ends so we can hand over access or move it to a new host and avoid downtime.

</TosSection>
