<TosSection id="commission-process" icon="clipboard-list" title="Commission Process">

### What to expect

- If you approve the quote for a project, a non-refundable deposit will be expected upfront.
- If your art request is approved, payment will be expected upfront.
- I will share my progress at various stages of completion. In the early stages (sketch, color, wireframing, mockups, placeholders), you are allowed to request any changes at each phase. Once approval to finalize (shading, details, usable assets, dev instance), only minor changes can be requested.
- You may ask for a progress update anytime, within reason.
- For Web or Discord Applications, you are expected to maintain and host the project yourself after completion.

### My Rights

- I offer conditional refunds. [See my refund policy](#refund-policy).
- In most cases, you will only receive the digital copy of the artwork. Shipping charges apply to physical works you wish to receive. If you would like to print physical media of your commission yourself, see [usage rights](#usage-policy).
- I retain the right to refuse or cancel a commission.
- I retain the right to post your commission or use it in my personal portfolios.
- I don’t accept commissions with deadlines or fast turnarounds.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="payment" icon="money-bill-wave" title="Payment">

- A complex OC design charge of $12 may be applied.
- For extra or re-occurring costs, I can offer ongoing maintenance or hosting for Web or Discord Applications I’ve developed.
- Payment for Web or Discord Applications requires a quote. This is discussed with you in a collaborative effort to understand the project scope and requirements. The cost is calculated for the time and complexity of completion. A deposit of 30% is required upfront. You will not be charged for overtime occurred on my half, however extra overtime costs might occur if the scope expands or changes.
- All other payments for commissions are required upfront. Payment plans can be offered to trusted, returning clients.
- All of the prices for my time are quoted or priced as CAD via PayPal or Ko-Fi.
- I reserve the right to change my prices at any point.
- I cannot apply further discounts to my prices.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="refund-policy" icon="receipt" title="Refunds">

- If I cancel a commission, you will be refunded a full or partial refund based on completion progress.
- Refund will be full if the illustration isn’t started, 75% if sketched/wireframed, 50% if lined/mocked-up, and 25% if flat coloured/working dev instance. You will receive the unfinished artwork or any assets I’ve created for you.
- Deposits for Web or Discord projects are non-refundable.
- You will be blacklisted from future commissions if you attempt to file a chargeback. I am happy to help resolve any issues or misunderstandings. Contact me if you have any concerns.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="usage-policy" icon="share-nodes" title="Re-posting and Usage">

- You may repost your commission online. My watermark must be present and clearly visible. You may use any part of your commission as an avatar. Credit via kokoneko.art is highly appreciated.
- Custom Web or Discord Applications do not require credit, though it is highly appreciated. Watermarks will not be present in this case.
- You may use your commission for private/offline use (phone/desktop background, to print on a sticker or poster, etc).
- You may not use your commission for profit or commercial use without clearly communicating your intent prior to commissioning me. This includes but not limited to: advertising, a book cover, logo etc.
- You may not use your commission for NFTs, or the advertising of NFTs.
- You may not use your commission to train AI, or to alter the work using AI.
- You may rehome a character I have designed you as a gift or trade. When reselling a character design, do not resell with the intent of profiting off of the design.
- You may redesign a character that I have designed.
- You may redesign or redevelop websites, code, or apps I’ve created.
- I retain the rights to use my artwork and code however I wish.

</TosSection>
